One image summing up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026: 31 stories
across 6 regions — COP31's deal at dawn in Antalya, the G20's first summit
on African soil in Johannesburg, Bitcoin's 8% rebound — plus a stat strip,
a UTC timeline, a world map and a DATE CHECK box listing the big stories
that happened on earlier days and were deliberately left out.

`main.py` (standard library only) copies the pre-rendered `entry.png`
(1024×1024) to the output directory. `build.py` + `draw.py` draw the page
with PIL and the Lexend font (Google Fonts, OFL) committed under `fonts/`.
